About

Born in LA, shaped by Chicago, finishing up school in Austin. Motion designer and 3D artist — self-taught through YouTube and a love for anything visual.

My work spans motion graphics, 3D visualization, and concept design — built in Cinema 4D, After Effects, and Blender. I've created visuals for DJ sets and conceptualized campaigns for brands like Under Armour and Nike.

At Verge Aero I work as a creative manager and show director — taking drone shows from the first client conversation through design, choreography, and live flight. I sit between the client and the production team, translating creative briefs into shows that actually fly.

I'm also deep in the AI toolchain — training ComfyUI fashion LoRAs and generating brand imagery, pairing TouchDesigner with StreamDiffusion for real-time visuals, and writing software that streamlines creative pipelines.

I'm not precious about style — dark and gritty or clean and commercial — and I move fast. Off-screen, I play goalie for the Texas Longhorns hockey team: same problem-solving instinct, just with a stick instead of a stylus.
